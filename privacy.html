Privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Who uses Jarvis

Jarvis is a private personal assistant app that runs on its owner's own computer. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data Jarvis uses, and why

  • Google Calendar: to show the owner's events and to add events or reminders the owner asks for.
  • Google Drive (only files Jarvis creates): to save the owner's backups of Jarvis to the owner's own Drive. Jarvis cannot see any other files in Drive.

Where the data goes

Data stays on the owner's computer and in the owner's own Google account. Jarvis does not sell, share, rent or transfer Google user data to anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models. The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account › Security › Third-party access.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
